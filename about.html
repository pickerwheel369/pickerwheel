About Pickerwheel.live

Making decision-making fun, fair, and effortless for millions of teachers, gamers, creators, and teams around the globe.

Who We Are

Welcome to Pickerwheel.live, your go-to interactive online decision-making tool. We designed Pickerwheel.live out of a simple conviction: everyday choices shouldn't be stressful, and picking random winners should always be thrilling, visually engaging, and unequivocally fair.

Whether you are a teacher looking to call on students without bias, a social media influencer hosting a live prize giveaway, or a family debating what to have for dinner, our mission is to deliver an intuitive, distraction-free spinning experience right in your browser.

Our Core Promise: Pickerwheel.live will always remain 100% free to use, requiring no account creation, no password setup, and no personal data submissions. Fair decisions belong to everyone.

True Algorithmic Randomness

Fairness is the heartbeat of our platform. When you spin the wheel on Pickerwheel.live, the outcome isn't predetermined or biased by entry order. Here's how our engine ensures pure fairness:

  • Uniform Probability: Each wedge on the wheel is calculated with exact mathematical precision. If you input 10 choices, every single item has an exact 1-in-10 (10%) probability of winning.
  • Cryptographic Random Seed: We utilize the modern browser's high-entropy random number generators combined with dynamic angular deceleration physics.
  • Visual Physics Simulation: The wheel accelerates smoothly, coasts through dynamic rotations, and decelerates naturally with a physical pointer bounce. What you see is authentic simulated momentum.

Who Uses Pickerwheel.live?

Our versatile wheel spinner caters to a wide spectrum of creative and professional communities:

  • Educators & Classrooms: Teachers utilize our tool to randomly assign student speaking turns, choose group project partners, and gamify trivia quizzes.
  • Streamers & Content Creators: Twitch broadcasters, YouTubers, and Instagram creators rely on our live audio and confetti celebration effects to conduct transparent subscriber giveaways.
  • Remote Agile Teams: Scrum masters and product managers use our wheel for daily standup speaker ordering, sprint retro icebreakers, and task delegation.
  • Friends & Families: From game night penalties to choosing weekend getaway destinations, we eliminate the dreaded "I don't know, you pick" dilemma.

Privacy-First Philosophy

Unlike many modern web applications, Pickerwheel.live is built with a deep respect for your digital sovereignty. We do not require sign-ups, we do not harvest email lists, and your custom list choices stay safely stored in your own device's browser memory (Local Storage). When you close your browser, your choices remain yours alone.
